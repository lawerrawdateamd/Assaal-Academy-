# Assaal-Academy-
Assaal Academy is an online educational platform offering practical, career-focused courses in Excel, Power BI, Python, and other in-demand skills. It empowers professionals to develop job-ready competencies, enhance their performance, and unlock new career opportunities through expert-led training and hands-on learning.
